NEON DRIFT

Steer the ship. Thread the gates. Outrun the tunnel.

How to Play

  • Steer with the mouse / finger drag, or arrow keys / WASD. The ship has momentum — it banks into turns, so steer early.
  • Your ship flies forward on its own, faster the longer you survive. Dodge the violet blocks and mines, and the moving barrier walls — fly through the gap, never the wall.
  • Thread the magenta boost gates for speed and score. A gate refills your boost meter, so the fast line runs through the gates.
  • Collect gold energy cells for score. Skim close past an obstacle for a near-miss bonus — the moment briefly slows so you can enjoy it.
  • Hold Space (or the Boost button on touch) to burn boost — it drains the meter, so spend it where the tunnel is clear.
  • You have 3 shields. A hit costs one and stuns the ship; lose all three and the run ends. Reach the end of the track to complete it.
  • Score comes from distance, gates and cells. Your best distance is saved per track — beat it.

Controls

  • Mouse / touch: drag to steer · Space / Boost button: boost
  • Arrow keys / WASD: steer · P: pause · M: mute · R: restart
  • Esc closes a panel · Enter confirms on menus
